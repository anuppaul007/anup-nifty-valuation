# Implementation Cost & Tax Policy v1

**Status:** research implementation contract; zero live allocation authority.

This policy prevents implementation assumptions from becoming a second hidden optimizer after the valuation rule is chosen.

## 1. Trading friction

Primary assumption is **15 bps one-way on actual traded notional**, with 10 bps and 25 bps sensitivities. Costs are charged on the amount traded, not on the full portfolio every month.

A different primary friction assumption requires a new version before outcome inspection; it cannot be selected after seeing which value preserves the backtest.

## 2. Do not double-count embedded fund costs

Return-source accounting matters:

- If an **actual mutual-fund/ETF NAV total-return series** is used, its TER and ordinary tracking effects are already embedded in the observed NAV path. Do not subtract the same TER/tracking difference again.
- If a **clean index/benchmark total return** is used, the implementation layer must map it to a specified investable vehicle and add the costs that are not embedded in that benchmark.
- Bid/ask spread, brokerage/levies, slippage and investor-level taxes are separate from fund-level TER and must be modeled only when applicable to the chosen vehicle.

Every result must state which costs are embedded versus added explicitly.

## 3. There is no single Indian after-tax return

Tax depends on investor type, legal vehicle, holding period, realization path and historical law. Therefore every after-tax result must name a versioned tax profile rather than writing only “India tax.”

A valid taxable profile records at least:

- investor category;
- equity/debt implementation vehicle;
- historical effective dates for tax-rule changes;
- lot-accounting/realization convention;
- holding-period classification;
- grandfathering/transition treatment where applicable;
- loss set-off/carry-forward assumptions;
- surcharge/cess assumptions;
- dividend/distribution treatment;
- primary-source references for each rule period.

If any material period is not implemented, the after-tax series is labelled incomplete and cannot support promotion.

## 4. Lot-aware realization

Rebalancing taxes are path-dependent. The implementation engine must maintain a holdings/lot ledger rather than applying a flat annual tax haircut to returns.

At each trade it should be possible to reproduce:

- units sold;
- acquisition dates and tax basis;
- realized gain/loss;
- short/long holding classification under the applicable rule period;
- tax generated by the transaction;
- remaining lots and basis.

Any approximation must be declared before outcome comparison and tested as a sensitivity.

## 5. Reporting layers

Always publish:

1. gross/pre-friction strategy return;
2. net-of-trading-cost return;
3. net-of-embedded/vehicle implementation return, without double counting;
4. after-tax return for every claimed taxable profile;
5. annual turnover, tax paid, realized gains/losses and terminal wealth.

This decomposition should make it obvious whether an apparent timing edge came from valuation skill, lower equity beta, a favorable vehicle, or tax assumptions.

## 6. Promotion thresholds

The generic timing-efficacy claim remains governed by Institutional Validation Protocol v1 and must clear its **+0.80 pp/year** residual-CAGR hurdle after the primary non-tax implementation costs.

If the project claims that a strategy has meaningful value for a specific **taxable investor/vehicle profile**, the same **+0.80 pp/year** residual-CAGR hurdle must also survive that fully implemented after-tax profile. A favorable pre-tax result with a non-positive or sub-hurdle after-tax residual may still be reported as research, but it is not a validated taxable implementation.

A tax profile may not be chosen after outcomes are inspected because it makes one strategy look better.

## 7. Debt sleeve

The primary debt vehicle exists as low-duration defensive/dry-powder capital. Its selection must be based on predeclared credit quality, duration, continuity and investability—not on which candidate debt series gives the highest strategy CAGR.

A material change in debt vehicle or tax treatment creates a new implementation-regime ID so results from different regimes cannot be silently pooled.

## 8. Evidence boundary

A realistic cost/tax engine can invalidate a backtest; it cannot validate the valuation signal by itself. Passing this implementation policy is necessary for an investability claim but does not establish timing alpha.
