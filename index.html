What must be tested before changing the allocation rule?
  • Recover original release vintages, with each value available before its decision time.
  • Compare net total-return portfolios with NIFTY buy-and-hold, a fixed 60/40 portfolio and an exposure-matched static portfolio.
  • Report drawdown, CAGR, expected shortfall, time under water, turnover and timing-only edge together.
  • Test lump-sum deployment, monthly contributions and inflation-linked retirement withdrawals separately.
  • Compare short-duration debt with long-duration bonds, including instrument costs and applicable taxes.
  • Evaluate macro challengers and all attempted variants without automatically promoting a backtest winner.

These evaluations are ongoing, not proof. First process review: 12 September 2027. A review date or a small number of overlapping annual outcomes does not establish statistical validation.

Fixed model assumptions and limits

V3.13 uses a monotone log profitability-adjusted P/B lens, calibrated on the same frozen V3.10 sample. See the reliability review for the formula and limitations. P/B and implied-profitability references use medians and population standard deviations of 36 completed month-end observations, September 2023–August 2026. This limited sample is not a fair-value guarantee. V3.13 retains the earnings overlay and the one-way SMA10 risk brake; those policy controls do not retroactively validate the historical V3.6/V3.10 research samples.
